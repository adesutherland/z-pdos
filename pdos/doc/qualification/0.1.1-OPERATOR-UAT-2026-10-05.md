# z/PDOS 0.1.1 local operator acceptance — 5 October 2026

This record covers the current-source 0.1.1 candidate operated through the
Mainframe Lab managed `pdos` guest. At acceptance time it was not yet a tagged
or published release; the result belongs to the named source and guest inputs.
The [stage 3 record](STAGE3-2026-10-05.md)
owns the earlier CMS implementation result; the operator's dated Mainframe Lab
report and private receipts retain full traces and stopped-media comparisons.

## Exact candidate and repair

The producer was `develop` at `63342a3d2eb4c917472231e12743fbd6850ae84d`
with uncommitted 0.1.1 source changes. HEAD alone does not identify the build:
`build/pdos/0.1.1-uat-shutdown-fix/inputs.sha256`, its component source
manifests and `outputs.sha256` pin the exact inputs and outputs. The image
was built on macOS arm64 with Apple Clang 21.0.0, local cREXX
`1.0.0-beta.3+local.g5949ef27efd8` and Mainframe Classic C 3.4.6. The image
recipe built 23 fresh objects, checked all three linked programs at two bases,
and compared the compressed disk with a complete flat-disk readback. The
resulting bare `pdos00.cckd` SHA-256 is
`662e54b3f41880d68dfd468dd32f20e7d829dbedb1e2a98f4c5c8e9b7fea6192`.
Its `PLOAD.SYS`, `PDOS.SYS` and `COMMAND.EXE` SHA-256 values are respectively
`30a113f40ac31f919b8c7123dd6830980c45cb72f049dcdde611e4a9c50364e8`,
`a5116de5bc8590f2fca4efd32f896bc11b580638486c23c64a55e944ffcee3e7`
and `b5729c97af9d7ee106d861c2b86906335300a3054db41495b2996ef33cdcb9dd`.
The kernel banner and PCOMM `VERSION` report 0.1.1 from the same source header;
the host PCOMM check compares that output with root `VERSION`.

An earlier 0.1.1 candidate,
`f2bd11161caa323904633d8a513631364eeb4ac0acd9afbac57c97024c7b3008`,
passed its CMS workload and output checks but did not reach wait `0444` after
CMS24 and PCOMM EXIT. The fixed CMS24 image overlaps PLOAD's boot heap, and
the observed PSW mapped to PLOAD's C exit. The repaired z/Architecture kernel
loads its own enabled wait PSW after shutdown. A focused CMS24 rerun passed
`PASS=6 FAIL=0 SKIP=1`, RC 0, and observed wait `0444`; the complete operator
matrix below then ran on the repaired candidate. This fixes the observed
shutdown path, while [PD-003](../BACKLOG.md#pd-003-shared-selectors-and-native-64-bit-kernel)
still owns the memory-layout redesign.

## Managed selection and handback

After acceptance, the operator selected `versions/zpdos-0.1.1` as the managed
`pdos` `current` target and retired the previous `zpdos-0.1` copy. The
installed IPL disk SHA-256 is
`f48614f4243e98c252bbd1e67b545712c6e8c5a2fc805ae8dd017b31e0506701`;
the retained CMS exchange disk SHA-256 is
`6bdd36c309dd4d204bebaca190c26c5bbb7f70e0510dfee043346fc71b888c4f`.
The installed IPL disk differs from the bare producer image because the
operator retained the qualified HIGH tools and workspace. Stopped-disk
readback pinned the unchanged OS and native inputs in that working image.
The managed configuration attaches both disks at 01B9 and 01BA. The final
readiness boot mounted, selected and unmounted CMSX01, returned to PDOS00,
reached wait `0444`, and exited. Fleet status then showed `pdos` stopped with
no process or leases; no task-owned Hercules, s3270 or cREXX process remained.
Mainframe Lab's `docs/execution/LOCAL-PDOS.md` describes the selected working
image and its replacement procedure.
